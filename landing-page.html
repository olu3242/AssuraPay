How it works

Five steps, one custodian, zero custody for AssuraPay

The money never leaves a licensed rail. AssuraPay only decides — verifiably — when it's allowed to move.

01

Create

Payer or payee defines the amount and the condition that must be met before release.

02

Fund

Payer funds through Paystack, Flutterwave, or bank transfer into a licensed custodian account.

03

Verify

The condition is checked — document, dual sign-off, or attested delivery event.

04

Instruct

AssuraPay issues a signed release instruction to the custodian. It never touches the funds.

05

Settle

Custodian pays out. Every step is logged to an append-only, tamper-evident audit trail.

AssuraPay never holds your money — by design, not just policy.

Funds sit with a licensed custodian — a bank or PSP — from the moment they're sent to the moment they're released. AssuraPay issues signed release instructions only. There is no pooled account, no wallet, and no code path where AssuraPay takes custody.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does AssuraPay ever hold my money?+
No. Funds are always held by a licensed custodian — a partner bank or PSP such as Paystack or Flutterwave. AssuraPay issues release instructions; it has no pooled account and no signing authority over customer funds.
Which payment rails does AssuraPay support?+
Paystack, Flutterwave, and manual bank transfer at launch, routed through a pluggable rail adapter so more banks and processors can be added without changing how assurance requests work.
How is a release condition verified?+
Three ways: document upload with reviewer sign-off, dual approval from both parties, or an oracle/webhook attestation from a trusted source such as a logistics provider. AI assists with document pre-checks but never auto-approves a release above your configured threshold.
What happens if we disagree on whether the condition was met?+
Either party can raise a dispute, which immediately freezes any pending release instruction. The case routes to a named arbitration process with a full evidence trail — funds stay with the custodian until resolved.
Can AssuraPay handle split payments to multiple recipients?+
Yes. A single release event can disburse to multiple parties in one instruction — for example, seller payment plus platform fee plus referral share.
Is AssuraPay available outside Nigeria?+
AssuraPay launches NGN-first. Cross-border and multi-currency support is on the roadmap once the FX Conversion Engine and correspondent banking partnerships are in place.
