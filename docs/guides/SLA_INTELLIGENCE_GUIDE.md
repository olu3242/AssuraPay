# SLA Intelligence Guide

SLA items identify the contractual, milestone, review, approval or settlement window, due time, optional completion time and canonical progress. Completed work scores a breach only when completion is after its due time. Open overdue work has 100% breach probability; open future work combines remaining progress with time remaining. The result reports hours remaining, breach state, probability and Low/Medium/High late-completion risk.

Predictions guide review and do not amend deadlines or declare contractual breach as a legal decision.
