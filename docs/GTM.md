# Go-To-Market Strategy — AssuraPay

## 1. Positioning

**"Payments that only move when trust is verified."**
AssuraPay is the assurance layer African B2B trade, marketplaces, and
public-sector disbursement plug into — without switching payment rails and
without AssuraPay ever touching the money.

## 2. Sequencing

### Phase 1 — Prove it on SME B2B trade (Months 0–4)
- Target: Nigerian SME buyers/sellers doing recurring B2B trade (building
  materials, agro-commodities, wholesale/distribution) where pre-payment
  risk is a known pain point.
- Channel: direct outreach + content (LinkedIn carousels, case studies)
  under the Zenith AI content engine; warm intros through Olu's existing
  Salesforce/automation consulting network.
- Goal: 10–20 pilot transaction pairs live, PSP integration (Paystack)
  proven end-to-end.

### Phase 2 — Marketplace embed (Months 3–9)
- Target: 2–3 Nigerian marketplace platforms (agriculture, building
  materials, B2B wholesale) that want built-in buyer/seller trust without
  becoming a PSP themselves.
- Offer: embeddable SDK/widget, revenue share or subscription pricing.
- Goal: first white-label partner live in production.

### Phase 3 — Public-sector / DFI pilot (Months 6–15)
- Target: one state-level MDA or DFI-funded program needing auditable,
  milestone-based disbursement (aligns with open-contracting and
  anti-leakage initiatives).
- Offer: dedicated deployment, compliance/audit reporting tier.
- Goal: signed pilot MoU, documented audit trail case study.

### Phase 4 — Cross-border / diaspora trade (Months 12+)
- Target: diaspora-linked import/export trade where buyer and seller are
  in different countries and trust friction is highest.
- Requires: FX Conversion Engine maturity, correspondent banking
  partnerships.

## 3. Acquisition Channels

- **Content-led GTM** (consistent with Zenith AI's existing model):
  LinkedIn/TikTok carousels explaining escrow-without-custody, case
  studies from pilot transactions, positioned as pre-sales material.
- **Partnership channel**: PSPs (Paystack, Flutterwave) as distribution —
  AssuraPay as a value-added layer on top of their existing merchant base.
- **Direct sales**: marketplace and public-sector segments need direct,
  relationship-led sales given transaction size and compliance sensitivity.

## 4. Pricing (see BRD for full model)

- SME/B2B: per-transaction fee (0.5%–1.5%).
- Marketplace: tiered SaaS + revenue share.
- Public-sector/DFI: deployment fee + per-disbursement fee.

## 5. Competitive Framing

| Alternative | Why AssuraPay wins |
|---|---|
| Informal trust (advance payment, personal relationships) | Programmatic, auditable, doesn't rely on relationship capital |
| Fully custodial escrow startups | Lighter regulatory lane, faster to launch, no balance-sheet risk to the platform |
| Manual milestone sign-off (government/enterprise) | Faster, auditable, harder to dispute or manipulate |
| Building it in-house (marketplace) | Faster time-to-market via embeddable SDK vs. building escrow logic internally |

## 6. Key Risks to GTM

- PSP partnership dependency — mitigate by supporting manual bank-transfer
  escrow account as a fallback rail from day one.
- Public-sector sales cycles are long — Phase 1–2 commercial revenue must
  sustain the business independently of Phase 3 timing.
- Trust-building for a new brand in a trust-sensitive category — early
  case studies and transparent audit-trail demos are the core credibility
  asset.

## 7. Success Metrics by Phase

| Phase | Primary metric |
|---|---|
| 1 | # completed assured transactions, dispute rate |
| 2 | # active marketplace partners, embedded GMV |
| 3 | # public-sector pilots signed, audit reports delivered |
| 4 | # cross-border corridors live |
