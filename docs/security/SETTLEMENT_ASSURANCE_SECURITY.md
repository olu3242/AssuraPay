# Settlement Assurance Security

Every record is workspace-scoped and backed by RLS contracts. Every money field is validated as a positive integer in minor units (kobo); a financial entitlement can never confirm with a negative net payable, and once confirmed it is immutable. Invoicing rejects a duplicate invoice number outright and only auto-matches on an exact amount match against the confirmed entitlement — approval is unreachable without a match. Escrow and funding assurance (Engine 44) never asserts custody itself: a funding commitment stores only an external provider reference, and confirmation requires the caller-supplied `ExternalCustodyGateway` to independently report success; reservations are capped at the committed amount and can never oversubscribe a commitment. Conditional release orchestration (Engine 45) only evaluates and records whether a release request's conditions — eligibility, invoice approval, fund reservation — are met; it never authorizes or submits a payment instruction. No method in `packages/settlement-assurance` holds, pools, or has signing authority over funds; see `settlement-assurance.non-custody.test.ts` for the enforced constraint.
