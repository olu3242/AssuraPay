# Execution Health Model

All seven signals are caller-supplied canonical scores bounded from 0 to 100. Execution risk is inverted; higher risk lowers health. The weighted result is rounded and classified as Healthy (80–100), Watch (60–79), At Risk (40–59), or Critical (0–39). The score stores its source signals and weight set for explainability and replay.

The KPI is advisory. It cannot certify completion, establish payment eligibility or authorize settlement.
